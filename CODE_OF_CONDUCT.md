# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be curious, assume good faith, and criticise models and methods — never people. Many people here
are learning outside their own field; that is the point. Harassment or demeaning behaviour of any kind is not
welcome.

Report problems to the maintainers by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/dogum/ML-Ratchet/security/advisories/new) or contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports are handled confidentially.
