# FaultFinder v1.1 | Selecting a day from a longer log can change its diagnosis

**First pass. The public date selector applies sensor quality checks outside the requested day.** FaultFinder diagnoses a completed local day of trend readings and optionally uses the same unit's preceding days. The CLI accepts a date within a file. In `raw_day`, temperature channels are discarded as flatlined based on the range across the supplied frame. Selection of the requested date happens afterwards. [CLI](../../../../models/faultfinder/pipeline.py#L24) · [Flatline check and date selection](../../../../models/faultfinder/faultfinder.py#L83)

We held FLEXLAB's target-day readings fixed, with its supply temperature set to 18 °C to simulate a flatlined sensor. Passing that day alone or appending another day at 18 °C gave **P(fault) = 0.51336**. Changing only the appended day's supply temperature to 19 °C gave **0.49950**, crossing the 0.5 alarm threshold. All four packaged target days showed sensitivity under this stress. Their unmodified single-day controls were stable. Four genuine earlier/later same-unit file pairs also showed zero change when the later file was appended. The exam's Nehasil preparation performs daily quality control. How often the date-selector bug changes a real diagnosis remains unknown. [Exam quality control](../../../../models/faultfinder/training/exam2_common.py#L75)

**The shared wind-cache collision reaches FaultFinder's simulation path.** We ran the lab's healthy VAV office for a winter day under fixed synthetic weather, changing only the wind speed. A fresh 2 m/s run gave P(fault) = 0.46271. An 8 m/s run reusing that cache returned identical raw channels. After clearing the cache, the 8 m/s run moved supply temperature by up to 5.19 °C and P(fault) to 0.46677. The bank normally reuses one city weather series for paired healthy and faulty simulations. We found no evidence here of a mislabeled bank or of ThermoPilot's fitted building card entering the network. The effect on the released bank and exam has not been measured. [Lab simulation](../../../../models/faultfinder/training/labsim.py#L41) · [Bank weather source](../../../../models/faultfinder/training/simbank.py#L27) · [Cache key](../../../../shared/building/buildingsim/buildingsim/weather.py#L219)

**Reference dates and missing signals.** The four supplied reference files met the seven-day rule. Re-dating them two weeks later without changing the readings gave identical probabilities. The public API trusts the caller to enforce reference chronology. A full day with zero sensor readings produced P(fault) = 0.45255 and a ranked diagnosis. The four-unit Nehasil exam screens days for observed supply temperature and runtime. Its 513 days and the 20-day calibration set give different evidence about performance from this empty-input control. [Reader](../../../../models/faultfinder/faultfinder.py#L230) · [Calibration, twins and real exam](../../../../models/faultfinder/MODEL_CARD.md#L217)

All folded weight arrays were finite with zero low-norm output filters or rows. All three heads matched the training-side Torch model within 7.16e−7 on the four examples. The six v4 decisions replayed. On v5, the two scored reference candidates reproduced their decisions against the rescored incumbent. The remaining entries document a monotone calibration control and an informational retrain. R8 seeds Torch and trains only the extra reference weights. We did not rebuild the banks, fit a new temperature or regenerate the exam.
