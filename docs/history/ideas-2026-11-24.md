## Mental model: TreeTwin (for future reference)

When a neural network passes on a simulated twin and misses on the real unit, the twin test flags a simulator gap — but a neural net cannot directly name *which* physical input caused it. **TreeTwin** trains gradient-boosted trees (with monotone physics constraints, exported to pure numpy) on the same simulated bank, then computes **TreeSHAP** attributions on each real dev unit and its simulated twin:

$$\Delta \phi_j = \phi_j(\text{real}) - \phi_j(\text{twin})$$

Subtracting the two SHAP vectors yields an exact, per-feature decomposition of the sim-to-real mismatch (e.g., showing whether the gap lives in heating degree days, floor area, or wind speed) — turning the twin test into an automated simulator debugger, while adding `gbm` and Mondrian `conformal` error bars to `mlratchet`.
