# RoadSim physics v2, work package B2: thermal, HVAC, accessories, engine efficiency, battery aging

Written 13 Nov 2026. Code: `roadsim/thermal.py` (new), `roadsim/aging.py` (new), small hooks in `sim.py`, and per-run
state in `components.py` (Engine, Motor, Battery, Cabin). Tests: `tests/test_roadsim_b2.py`.

Every option is off by default. With all of them off RoadSim reproduces physics v1 exactly (fingerprint: 171 runs,
1e-9). The preset `roadsim-physics-v2-b2` switches them on with the values below. Every other tunable number is in
`thermal.PARAMS`, with its source, and `options['thermal_params']` overrides any of them. Nothing here was fitted to
EPA, VED or D3 data. The one derived number (`oil_fric_scale`) keeps v1's 24 C cold-start fuel on default and
generated cars (no measured data).

## What each option models

### Engine warm-up (gap 1, LIMITS 4)

- **Two nodes.** Coolant/block: v1's capacity and loss (`C_J_K` 12 kJ/K per L + 12 kJ/K, `hA` 8 W/K per L + 10 W/K),
  gets `(f_coolant - 0.07) (P_fuel - P_brake)`. Oil: `C = 6 kJ/K + 5 kJ/K per L` (oil ~4-5 L at 2.1 kJ/kg/K plus the
  crankshaft, sump and rods it wets; PROMETS, Shayler et al. 1993, SAE 931153), gets 7 % of `(P_fuel - P_brake)` and
  exchanges `8 W/K per L x (T_coolant - T_oil)` with the block; the sump loses `(1.5 + 0.25 v) W/K per L` to the air.
  The thermostat holds the coolant at `T_hot + 2`, the oil at `T_hot + 12`.
- **Friction on the oil.** `dT_fric = c_fric x oil_fric_scale x disp x g(T_oil) / g(20 C)`, `g = (nu / nu_hot)^0.5 - 1`
  (film friction ~ sqrt(viscosity); Patton, Nitschke & Heywood 1989). v1's `c_fric` (45 Nm/L at 20 C, fitted on dev
  bag 1 / bag 3 with the fast coolant node) lumps every cold loss. Moving it to the slower oil node with no rescale
  doubles the 24 C cold-start fuel; `oil_fric_scale = 0.33` keeps it (9 default and generated cars, chassis-dyno
  settings, UDDS at 23.9 C, with the axle and enrichment terms on: ratio 1.00, spread 0.94-1.10).
- **Vogel viscosity.** `nu = a exp(theta1 / (T + theta2))`, theta1 = 800 K, theta2 = 95 K (SAE 5W-30: 65 cSt at 40 C,
  10.5 cSt at 100 C). Relative to 88 C it gives 13x at 20 C and 112x at -7 C; v1's Arrhenius law gives 11x and 42x. ATF
  uses theta1 = 706 K (transmission spin losses ~ viscosity^0.3, v1 form).
- **Enrichment.** Fuel x `(1 + a exp(-t_run / 40 s) + b)`, `a = cold_enrich x max(0, 25 C - T_coolant at start)`,
  `b = warmup_enrich x max(0, 20 C - T_coolant)`. Both are defined relative to the 24 C start, which v1's fitted friction
  already contains, so they act only in the cold. The unburnt fuel is booked as engine loss.

### Axle and tyres in the cold (gap 1)

- **Axle.** Drag at the wheels `T = (a0 + a1 w) (phi(T_axle) - 1)`, `phi = (nu / nu_80C)^0.3` with gear-oil Vogel
  theta1 = 854 K (75W-90). Car: a0 = 1.2 Nm, a1 = 0.025 Nm s per driven axle, scaled by (mass/1600)^0.7. Only the cold
  excess is added (the warm part is in `driveline_eff`). The axle oil node (1.2e4 J/K x (mass/1600)^0.8, hA 2 + 0.6 v
  W/K) is heated by its own churning. The force is booked in the `rolling` ledger term.
- **Tyres.** One node per vehicle: `C = 70 kJ/K x (m/1600)^0.85`, area 2 m2 x (m/1600)^0.6, h = 8 + 4.5 v^0.8 W/m2/K,
  heated by the rolling-resistance power, cooled to the road (ambient, plus 0.015 K per W/m2 of sun on the road).
  `Crr x exp(-0.008 (T_tyre - T_ref))`, clipped 0.7-1.8, with T_ref the steady tyre temperature at 22 m/s and 20 C
  (23.9 C on a dyno). Pressure follows the tyre temperature (set cold at 20 C). This replaces v1's ISO ambient factor
  and the 15 % warm-up factor. On a chassis dyno (`temp_effects: False`) only `tire_dyno_frac` = 60 % of Crr follows the
  tyre temperature (the dyno absorbs the rest at its set coefficients).

### Cabin and HVAC (gaps 2 and 3, LIMITS 5 and 18)

- **Two nodes.** Air + light trim (17 % of the spec `C_J_K`) and interior mass (67 %), coupled by 150 W/K x UA/120.
  80 % of the sun lands on the interior. The spec `UA_W_K` is split: 60 % conduction, the rest becomes explicit air
  flows: leakage 0.012 kg/s x UA/120 x (1 + 0.03 v) and the fresh-air share of the blower flow.
- **Air side.** Blower flow up to 0.13 kg/s (car; bus 2.2 kg/s), level = 0.25 + |T_cab - T_set| / 15 K; electrical power
  `2 x blower_W x level^2.5`. The controller asks for the supply temperature that brings the air node to the set
  point in 120 s. Recirculation: 0.3 heating, 0.8 cooling, 1.0 during pull-down (> 3 K above set), 0 in defrost.
  Engine-heated cars hold the blower at minimum until the coolant reaches 35 C (the D3 Accord LX does this for ~250 s).
- **Cooling.** Moist-air evaporator load `m (cp (T_mix - T_ev) + h_fg (w_mix - w_sat(T_ev)))`. Belt systems hold the
  evaporator at 4 C and reheat from the coolant; electric compressors run the evaporator at the supply temperature.
  Capacity 1.3 x `cool_max_W`, and for a belt compressor x (rpm / 1800)^0.8 (engine off: no cooling). COP = 0.33
  (belt) or 0.36 (electric) x Carnot between the refrigerant (T_ev - 6 K) and the condenser (ambient + 12 K + 13 K
  exp(-v / 8 m/s)): about 1.5 idling and 1.9 at speed at 35 C. Belt efficiency 0.95. Condenser fan `2 x blower_W x
  max(0.25, 1 - v / 20 m/s)` while the compressor runs. SC03 humidity default 40 % (100 grains/lb at 35 C).
- **Heating.** Engine coolant through the heater core (effectiveness 0.8). PTC: a coolant loop (16 kJ/K, 4 W/K loss)
  held at the needed temperature by a heater of `ptc_max_W` (car 5 kW, as the 2011-12 Leaf; SUV 6, van 7, bus 24 kW;
  spec `aux.HVAC.ptc_max_W`, `ptc_type: 'coolant' | 'air'`) whose power falls to zero over the 20 K below its 85 C switch
  temperature. Heat pump: v1's COP curve, capacity 35 % of `heat_max_W` at -15 C rising 2.5 %/K to 100 %, PTC backup.
  Seat heaters (75 W) + wheel (50 W) let the air set point drop 2 K. Defrost forces fresh air and runs the compressor
  above 3 C.
- **Hybrids' heat request** (`need_heat`, which B1's strategies act on): as v1, the cabin needs more than 30 % of
  `heat_max_W` and the coolant is below 55 C; with `phev_electric_heat` the PTC's capacity is subtracted first, so a PHEV
  starts for heat only when the heater cannot cope.
- **Battery heater.** `batt_heat_T_on` moves the pack heater's switch-on temperature (v1 BEV default: 3 kW below 0 C,
  which is most of the VED Leaf twin's 7.6 kW at cold stops vs 1.4 kW real; a 2013 Leaf only has a small warmer).

### Engine efficiency (gap 4, LIMITS 1 and 20)

Three knobs for the calibration pass, global or per map family: `eng_eta_scale` (all loads alike), `eng_lowload_W_per_L`
(an extra fuel power at idle that fades to zero at 4 kW/L brake power: the city/highway tilt) and `eng_dfmep_bar`
(friction; it moves FTP and HWFET almost alike, so it is not a tilt knob). `fead_drag_Nm` is a belt/accessory drag.
Measured on two dev cars (FE change, hot procedures as in epa_procedures):

| option | Camry LE FTP | Camry LE HWFET | F150 2WD FTP | F150 2WD HWFET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eng_dfmep_bar` 0.2 | -4.7 % | -4.0 % | -3.6 % | -3.5 % |
| `eng_eta_scale` 1.04 | +3.7 % | +4.0 % | +3.8 % | +4.0 % |
| `eng_lowload_W_per_L` 300 | -1.9 % | -0.4 % | -1.4 % | -0.3 % |
| lowload 300 + eta 1.05 | +2.6 % | +4.5 % | +3.2 % | +4.7 % |
| `fead_drag_Nm` 1.0 | -1.2 % | -1.0 % | -0.6 % | -0.6 % |

So `eta_scale` and `lowload` span the tilt (FTP +5.6 % / HWFET -4.3 % median needs roughly lowload ~900 W/L with
eta ~1.06; to be fitted on dev).

### Heavy duty (gap 5, LIMITS 9 and 10)

- **Buses** (`bus_aux`, class `bus` only) replace the lumped `base_W` by: 24 V loads 2.5 kW (alternator 55 % on diesels,
  DC/DC on electrified buses); an air compressor (tank 600 kJ in compressor-work units, cut-in at half; 60 kJ per stop
  for doors and kneeling, 12 kJ per friction-brake application, 150 W leakage; engine-driven 8 kW loaded at 1800 rpm and
  0.6 kW unloaded drag, or electric 6.5 kW); power steering (engine-driven 0.8 kW, electro-hydraulic 0.25 kW / 0.85, plus
  1.2 kW below 4 m/s while moving); 1.5 kW of e-drive cooling pumps and fans on hybrid and battery buses. With
  `hvac_model='v2'`, doors open 2 s into each stop: stack exchange `n (Cd / 3) A sqrt(g H dT / T)` + 0.15 m/s per door
  (2 doors, 2.4 m2, 2 m, Cd 0.6), about 1 kg/s at 15 K.
- **Large motors.** `motor_size_exp` scales the losses of machines above 150 kW by (P/150 kW)^-0.1 (larger machines are
  relatively more efficient). `motor_staging` treats a machine above 300 kW as N equal units; at light load the
  controller runs the fewest units that carry the torque, idle units keep 40 % of their zero-torque loss (iron and
  windage). Both lower losses, so both move the Class 8 BEV toward the literature.

### Battery aging (gap 6, LIMITS 17)

`aging.soh(chem, years, efc, T_C, soc_mean, dod)` -> capacity and resistance relative to new. NMC/NCA/LMO: Schmalstieg et
al. 2014 (J. Power Sources 257, 325) calendar (t^0.75, Arrhenius) and cycling (sqrt of charge throughput) terms; LFP:
Wang et al. 2011 (J. Power Sources 196, 3942) cycling plus a sqrt(t) calendar term; NiMH: linear. Cell-lab rates are
scaled (calendar 0.5, cycling 0.3) to ~2 %/year for an NMC EV at 25 C and 100 cycles/year, the fleet average in
telematics studies (Geotab: ~1.8-2.3 %/year). Examples: NMC 3 y / 300 cycles 0.92 capacity, 1.14 resistance; 10 y / 1500
cycles 0.82, 1.48; LFP 10 y / 1500 cycles 0.94, 1.11. The SOH scales the pack's Ah, energy and both resistances for the
run (SOC moves faster, losses and voltage sag grow, power limits fall). `Battery.usable_kWh(T)` gives the energy between
the SOC window's ends at C/3 with the resistive drop and voltage floor, so cold and aged packs deliver less.

The BEV bus moves into the measured range. The hybrid's advantage drops from +76 % to +60 %: still too good. The

`hvac_seat_heat`, the spec's `heat_on_below_C`, `batt_heat_T_on`) for the calibration pass to fit on VED dev Leaf trips.

### Look at Argonne D3 data (form only, nothing fitted)

Files present on 13 Nov: 2018 Honda Accord LX (complete), 2017 F150, 2018 Camry XLE, 2020 Bolt (partial, warm tests
only so far) and older cars. From the Accord LX (1.5T CVT) summary and 10 Hz traces:
- 20 F cold FTP (heater auto 72 F, fresh air): UDDS bag 1 26.5 mpg, bag 2 32.1 mpg vs 37.4 / 37.8 at 72 F (fuel x1.41 and
  x1.18). Same car hot at 20 F: 36.5-37.1 mpg vs 40.4-41.0 at 72 F (fuel x1.10). In a generic look-alike spec (not this
  car's map) v1 gives x1.26 / x1.07 and v2-b2 x1.31 / x1.12: the cold penalty grows but stays below the real one.
- Oil (dipstick) at -7 C: 16 C at 300 s, 29 C at 505 s, 42 C at 900 s, 49 C at 1372 s. The v2 oil node: 47, 62, 75, 80 C.
  The sump oil warms about twice as slowly as the model's oil node (the dipstick reads the coldest oil); worth fitting.
- Driven tyres: -2 C at 60 s, +9.5 C at 300 s, ~10-10.6 C after 900 s; model 5.5 C at 300 s, 14-17 C late. Same form.
- Blower held at level 1-2 of 7 for ~250 s, then 7; fresh air; A/C clutch off at -7 C. Matches the v2 logic.
- SC03 (35 C, 850 W/m2, auto 72 F): recirculation, vent air 7-13 C, compressor on ~100 %, cabin 45 -> 33 C in 120 s, 27 C
  at 300 s, 24 C at 600 s. Fuel with A/C auto vs A/C off: x1.30. On the dev Sonata's SC03 the sim gives x1.13 (v1) and
  x1.18 (v2), with the cabin at 33 C at 120 s and 28 C at 595 s: A/C is still too cheap and the pull-down too slow at the
  end, so COP and capacity are first in line for calibration on D3 dev cars.
- Hot (95 F) cold-start UDDS with auto A/C: 30.7 mpg vs 37.6 at 72 F.

## Ledger, speed, fingerprint

- Fingerprint: matches v1 (171 runs, 1e-9); `tests/test_roadsim_fingerprint.py` passes.
- Ledger stress: 245 runs (240 generated vehicles + 5 heavy-duty references; UDDS, HWFET, US06, LA92, NYCC, SC03,
  CBD14, HD transient; -20 to 40 C; grades +-4 %; sun, wind, surfaces; random option subsets incl. the preset, aging,
  engine adjustments, defrost, seat heat), run twice with different option draws. None above 5e-4; worst 2.1e-4 and
  5.3e-5, both on CVT cars where v1 itself gives 1.9e-4 and 5.3e-5 on the same run (the existing CVT residual, not B2). Every new loss is in an existing ledger term: enrichment in `eng_loss`, axle
