BasinDetective / Hydrology

What does a river’s record say about a decade it has not seen?

The network reads nine years of a basin’s weather and streamflow, plus the weather of the decade before. It says how six flow signatures changed, whether a reservoir sits upstream, and four hidden properties. Pick a real exam basin, then change the input: a wetter or warmer decade, a reservoir on the river, a coarser gauge. Every change recomputes 150 features and reruns five networks in your browser.

The honest part

What it adds, and what it does not

On the change between decades it is level with a GR4J model calibrated on each basin on five signatures and behind it on mean flow, and it is ahead of the plain “no change” answer on mean flow, Q95 and half-flow date. Its median errors on BFI and Q5 are no better than “no change”. What it adds is no per-basin calibration and an error bar on every answer; the bars are too wide (73–83 % of exam truths fall inside the 68 % bars). The weather data caps every method: ERA5-Land under-catches mountain and Pacific Northwest precipitation and drifts about 6 % between decades.

Where it fails

Reservoirs did not transfer

On simulated basins it spots a reservoir at AUC 0.91. On real basins it is at chance: AUC 0.49 against HydroATLAS storage, with 37 regulated basins. Simulated reservoirs follow generic operating rules, CAMELS basins are chosen to be little regulated, and HydroATLAS volumes can belong to a larger river. Try the reservoir slider above: a smooth store does not always raise its alarm.

The ratchet · WaterSim physics v1 → v4

Seven rungs, two rejected

Keep a rung if its skill on the frozen simulated set of the current physics beats the incumbent by more than 0.01 and the real dev composite (51 dev basins with a 1980s record) drops by no more than 0.02. Physics v2–v4 came from dev twins: gauge texture, precipitation bias, decadal drift, year-to-year error.

Forcing and flow: Caravan v1.6 (Kratzert et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0) with CAMELS-US (Newman et al. 2015; Addor et al. 2017) streamflow and ERA5-Land forcing (Muñoz-Sabater et al. 2021; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information). Regulation truth: HydroATLAS (Linke et al. 2019, CC BY 4.0) with GRanD volumes (Lehner et al. 2011); USGS NWIS peak codes (public domain). The four basins are the release’s examples: one fixed seeded draw per climate stratum plus one regulated basin, never chosen by score.
