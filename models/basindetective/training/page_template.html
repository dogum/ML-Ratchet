BasinDetective

Give it nine years of a river's gauge record and the weather of another decade. It says how that river's flow will differ in the other decade, whether a reservoir sits upstream, and what the basin hides underground, each with an error bar. It was trained only on simulated basins and graded on 619 real ones.

Read a basin

Pick a basin from a fixed random draw of exam basins (seeded, within climate strata, never chosen by score). The network read only the blue decade and the weather of the ochre one. Each card shows its answer (blue, with a 1-sigma bar), what the gauge really recorded (ochre tick) and the calibrated GR4J model (violet tick).

      Use it

      basindetective-v1.0.zip holds a numpy/scipy inference package, the weights, a demo on four real exam basins, the model card and every training script.

      import basindetective as bd
det = bd.load()
res = bd.predict(det, cal, q_cal, tgt)   # cal/tgt: daily P, T_mean, PET; q_cal: mm/day
res['target_period']['q_mean']           # calibration value, prediction, 68 % range
res['regulation']['p_regulated']
