FIREPILOT ML Ratchet · Wildfire phase 2 · Trained on physics. Graded on reality.

Containment-line planning · 72 h · real exam fires in FireSim

Where should crews cut line in the first three days of a fire?

FirePilot is a small neural network that ranks containment-line plans. It learned only from simulated fires in FireSim. It was then graded once on 48 real exam fires: their real LANDFIRE fuels and terrain, their real HRRR weather and their real first satellite detection, with the outcome scored in the simulator.

      Use it

      firepilot-v1.0.zip holds the numpy planner (firepilot.py, numpy and scipy only), the weights, demo.py on real exam fires (a few seconds), pipeline.py for your own fire with FireSim, the model card and every training script.

      unzip firepilot-v1.0.zip && cd firepilot-v1.0
python3 demo.py
# your own fire (needs FireSim for the forecast run)
from pipeline import plan_fire
plan, info, ctx = plan_fire(landscape, forecast_weather, burned_mask, start_utc, lon, rate_m_per_h=600)

      Data and credits

      FEDS-MTBS fire progression (Chen et al. 2022 method; Zenodo 20187963, CC BY 4.0). MTBS perimeters (USGS/USFS, public domain). LANDFIRE fuels, canopy and terrain (public domain). HRRR analyses via the University of Utah hrrrzarr archive on AWS Open Data (NOAA). gridMET daily weather (Abatzoglou 2013). Physics: FireSim (Rothermel 1972 with Albini 1976, Scott and Burgan 2005 fuel models, Van Wagner 1977 crown fire, Nelson dead-fuel moisture, minimum-travel-time spread). Line-holding limit from the NWCG fire characteristics ("hauling") chart: flame lengths above 8 ft (2.4 m) defeat hand and dozer line. All results are simulated outcomes on real inputs.
