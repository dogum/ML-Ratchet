AirfoilNet

An airfoil's lift, drag and moment curves from its shape and Reynolds number, with error bars. It learned only from an XFOIL-class flow solver, and it is graded on real wind-tunnel polars from the UIUC low-speed tests.

The lab: shape an airfoil, read its polar

Pick a tested airfoil or a library shape, then drag the handles on the surface to push the skin in or out. The network runs in this page on every change. The shaded band is its 68% error bar (already widened on the dev airfoils).
