LawFinder v1.0, ML Ratchet B batch, 22 Nov 2026. Simulators: WaterSim, FireSim, GridSim and BuildingSim (the collection's shared platforms, used unchanged). Networks compared: FlowCast, FireCast, GridPulse.

Data. Caravan / CAMELS-US (CC BY 4.0, Kratzert et al. 2023; Newman et al. 2015) with ERA5-Land forcing; USGS NWIS (public domain). FEDS-MTBS fire progression (CC BY 4.0, Chen et al. 2022), MTBS, LANDFIRE, HRRR, gridMET (US public data). NESO 1 s frequency, outturn inertia and generation mix (NESO Open Data Licence); Elexon BMRS REMIT/B1610 loss labels via GridData. EIA RECS 2020 (public domain); TMYx weather (climate.onebuilding.org). Splits are each platform's frozen splits, copied in LawFinder's splits.json.
