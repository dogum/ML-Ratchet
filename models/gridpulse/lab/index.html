GridPulse

When a power station or an interconnector trips, the whole grid's frequency drops at once. GridPulse reads the first seconds of that 1 s frequency record and estimates how many MW were lost, how much spinning inertia the grid had, how stiffly it responds, and where the frequency will bottom out and settle.

Ratchet

The ladder of rungs

A rung stays only if it beats the incumbent by more than 0.01 on a frozen set of simulated events (mean skill over MW lost, inertia and MW/Hz at 60 s, nadir and settling forecasts at 10 s) and does not make the four GB dev events more than 10 % worse. Skill is 1 − error / error of guessing the median.

    Use it

    The release

    numpy + scipy only. The window is 81 one-second samples, 20 s before the onset to 60 s after, as deviations from the 60 s pre-event mean.

    from gridpulse import GridPulse
gp = GridPulse()                                   # loads gridpulse_weights.npz
r = gp.read(t, f, system='GB', demand_MW=33000, N=10)   # t [s], f [Hz] at 1 s; onset found like NESO events
r['loss_MW'], r['loss_MW_lo'], r['loss_MW_hi']     # 1-sigma band (dev-widened)
r['E_GWs'], r['beta_MW_Hz'], r['nadir_Hz'], r['settle_Hz']
python3 demo.py                                    # six real exam events in a few seconds
