# RoomSense demo audio

The four clips use generated claps convolved with the measured Surrey Room A or B impulse response. The first ear channel was selected. The recordings include an independently selected, resampled and scaled ESC-10 background excerpt.

- Room responses by Cal Armstrong, Institute of Sound Recording, University of Surrey. The data were published with the [RealRoomBRIRs repository](https://github.com/IoSR-Surrey/RealRoomBRIRs) and its accompanying [MIT license](LICENSE-Surrey.txt).
- ESC-10 excerpt selection follows [ESC-50 by Karol J. Piczak](https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50#license). Original sounds used here are “Rain very heavy with thunder” by [Danjocross](https://freesound.org/people/Danjocross/sounds/164206/) (CC0), “Rain and Storm” by [josephsardin](https://freesound.org/people/josephsardin/sounds/177250/) (CC0), and “Loopable Ticking Clock” by [OwlStorm](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/212181/) (CC BY). The resulting excerpts are distributed with attribution.
