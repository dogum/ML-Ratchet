FireDetective

Give it a wildfire's satellite record: the burned area every half day, the VIIRS fire pixels and their radiative power, the weather and the fuel map. It tells you what drove the fire: how much of it burned as crown fire, how windy it was, how wet the live fuels were. It never saw a real fire while learning. Every answer below is graded against a measurement it did not use.

The ratchet

Every rung, kept or not

A rung stays only if it beats the incumbent by more than 0.01 on a frozen simulated validation set (mean R² of crown fraction, blind wind and live moisture) and does not lose more than 0.05 on the dev composite (Spearman ρ on 40 real dev fires). When the physics changed, the incumbent was re-scored on the new frozen set first.

How it learned

Simulated fires, seen through a simulated satellite

Twin test: where the simulator and reality disagree

Each of the 40 dev fires was re-run in FireSim from its first mapped perimeter, with its own fuels and weather, then seen through the same satellite and perimeter emulators. The table compares the inputs the network sees.
